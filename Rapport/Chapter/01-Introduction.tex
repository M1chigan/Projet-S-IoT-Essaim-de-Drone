Our project therefore lies within that application of utilizing drone swarms for carrying loads. It aims to implement drone swarms techniques based on Ultrawide Band technology (UWB) and Xbee modules. The first objective of the project is to get familiar with the hardware available and then work out algorithms to make multiple drones fly collectively. 

Two main questions emerge from reflecting on the implementation of a drone swarm. To behave collectively, each drone has to be aware of its positioning relatively to the others (or at least the neighbouring drones). Moreover, it must be necessary for them to communicate with each other. Therefore, a technological choice has to be made to fulfil these two necessities. To do so, we have been imposed to work with two technologies (UWB and Xbee) and therefore, we will study how they can be helpful for responding to these problematic points.
