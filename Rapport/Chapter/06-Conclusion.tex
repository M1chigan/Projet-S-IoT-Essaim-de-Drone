\chapter*{Conclusion}
\markboth{Conclusion}{Conclusion}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Conclusion}

To conclude on this first intermediate report we can say that, so far, we have made good progress in many different aspects of the project. We have
successfully familiarise ourselves with the hardware, both the drones and the UWB modules. We have made significant bibliographical studies on multiple themselves
that helped us get an overview of the different reasearch, from communication protocol to data processing. Visualisation and simulation tools have been developed
to prepare future outdoor tests and get a nice environement to work with. The next steps of the project will consist in finishing the bibliographical study, an IEEE-inspired article to 
get a clean overview of the project, formation control simulation and most importantly outdoor tests to finally see how our system behaves in reality.
